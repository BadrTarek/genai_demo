Invoice Approval Requirements

The system must support a two-level invoice approval workflow.

Invoices below 1,000 EUR require approval from one finance employee.

Invoices from 1,000 EUR up to 10,000 EUR require approval from a finance manager.

Invoices above 10,000 EUR require approval from both a finance manager and a department director.

The invoice creator cannot approve their own invoice.

An approver must be notified by email when an invoice is waiting for approval.

Approvers must be able to approve or reject an invoice.

A rejected invoice must include a rejection reason.

The system must record the approval decision, approver, timestamp, and comments.

Approved invoices cannot be edited unless they are reopened by a finance manager.
